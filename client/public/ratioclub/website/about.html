About Us

Bridging Cybernetic Rigor with Production AI Engineering on AWS

AI infrastructure is often sold on vendor promises and bought on blind trust. Enterprise buyers rarely have a way to independently verify interconnect performance, GPU throughput, or cost per token before capital is committed.

RatioClub is the independent engineering firm closing that gap—embedding senior Forward Deployed Engineers (FDEs) who bring architectural neutrality, empirical validation, and hands-on execution to production AI systems.

The RatioClub Standard — Contractual Neutrality & Senior Execution

Unlike traditional IT staffing agencies that bill open-ended hours with junior delivery benches, or hardware vendors motivated to sell oversized compute instances, RatioClub writes architectural neutrality into every client agreement.

Principal-Led Engagements

Every deployment is spearheaded by senior AWS infrastructure architects and systems engineers with hands-on cluster experience.

Vendor-Agnostic Verification

We validate multi-node topologies, network fabrics (EFA), and inference frameworks against your workload’s actual requirements—not vendor sales quotas.

Client Asset Ownership

You retain complete ownership of all raw benchmark datasets, deployment scripts, Terraform/IaC modules, and acceptance verification artifacts.

Our Heritage — From Cybernetics to Modern Enterprise AI

The name RatioClub pays direct homage to the historic 1949 British dining society of pioneering scientists, mathematicians, and engineers—including Alan Turing, W. Ross Ashby, and William Grey Walter—who gathered outside traditional academia to explore how brains and machines compute.

Today, we bring that same rigorous founding spirit to technical leaders building modern production AI systems. We reject black-box assertions in favor of verifiable benchmarks, transparent math, and reproducible cloud infrastructure.

Our Operating Principles (The FDE Way)

Code Over Slides

We do not deliver 80-page advisory decks. Forward Deployed Engineers sit alongside your platform and ML engineers to write code, configure hardware, and ship infrastructure.

Outcome-Based Accountability

We tie commercial terms directly to measurable operational targets—cost per token, throughput per watt, and system uptime.
